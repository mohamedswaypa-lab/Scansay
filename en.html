Getting started

How do we get started?

Apply on the website and choose a package. We send an offer within one working day. Once you approve it we open the account, you add your places and print the stickers the same day.

Does the customer need to download an app?

No. The phone camera opens the form straight in the browser. Anyone who cannot scan can type the code printed under the QR code.

Do you print the stickers for us?

No. You create the stickers in the dashboard, pick size and colour and print them yourselves on a normal printer or at your print shop.

Can we change the text on the sticker?

Yes. The heading at the top is free, for example your business name. The bottom text is “Leave feedback” by default but can be changed. “Powered by ScanSay” appears small at the bottom.

Which industries does ScanSay suit?

Any with customers. We have templates for 30 industries in six categories, from hotels, buses and taxis to libraries, gyms and shops. If your industry is missing we make a template.

Price and contract

What does it cost?

The price depends on the number of places, departments and features. You receive it in an offer after applying. The more units, for example hotels, the bigger the discount.

Can we pay monthly or annually?

Yes, both. Annual payment gives a lower price. It is stated in the offer.

Is there a lock-in period?

The contract period is stated in the offer. After that you can terminate no later than one month before the period ends.

Customers and the form

What happens when a customer sends a message?

The message lands in the inbox with place, topic and rating. If it is urgent the right person is alerted at once. The customer gets a copy of their message by email if they want.

Does the customer get a copy of their message?

Yes. The customer ticks the box and gets a copy by email with a button to confirm the address. In the dashboard you see which messages come from a confirmed address.

How do you stop bots and fake submissions?

The form has several protections the customer does not notice: a hidden field only bots fill in, a check of how fast the form is filled in, a hidden calculation in the browser, a limit on submissions, a stop for duplicates and links, and checks of name and email. Temporary throwaway addresses do not work, and misspelt addresses like gmial.com are corrected. The same checks run again on the server. No captcha is needed.

Can the customer stay anonymous?

Name is optional. Email is needed so the customer can get a copy and a reply, but it is only shown to the person handling the case, never to staff.

Which languages are available?

Swedish and English. The form follows the phone’s language and the customer can switch.

Reviews and rules

Does ScanSay affect our reviews on Google and Tripadvisor?

We never block, filter or steer public reviews. Every customer is invited the same way. What affects your rating is that you fix more problems in time.

Can we see which employee received complaints?

Not by default. No staff names appear in the form. Statistics per employee are unlocked only after consultation under MBL and are never used for automatic decisions.

Is ScanSay an emergency service?

No. In an emergency the customer should call 112. The form shows this when the customer picks topics such as safety.

Is this review gating?

No. Every customer gets the same invitation, happy or not, and we never steer anyone to or away from Google or Tripadvisor. ScanSay is operational follow-up per location as a complement to public reviews, not a replacement.

Will staff feel monitored?

Customers never see names. Statistics per employee are switched on only after an impact assessment, information and MBL consultation. Each employee sees their own feedback, praise counts as much as complaints and AI makes no decisions about staff.

Data and security

Where is the data stored?

With suppliers inside the EU/EEA. Personal data is deleted after 12 months by default.

Do you use cookies?

No tracking cookies and no advertising. Only what is needed for language choice and spam protection.

Do we get a data processing agreement?

Yes. The data processing agreement is included in every package and can be read on the website.

What if someone writes something illegal or offensive?

Text with staff names, sensitive details or threats is flagged automatically and reviewed before it is shown. It can be masked or deleted. Threats can be reported to the police by the business.

Who owns the data?

The business owns its feedback and can export it at any time. ScanSay only processes it on the business's behalf under the data processing agreement.

What happens after 12 months?

Messages with text, email and photo are deleted. Anonymous statistics without personal data are kept for 3 years so certificates can be checked.

Is the form accessible to everyone?

Yes, it is built for WCAG 2.1 AA: contrast, large buttons, labels, keyboard, screen readers and reduced motion.

1. Parties and background

This agreement is between the customer the customer named in the offer as controller (”the Customer”) and ScanSay as processor (”ScanSay”). It forms part of the main agreement (the Terms). In case of conflict on personal data matters, this agreement prevails.

2. Subject matter, nature and purpose

ScanSay processes personal data on the Customer’s behalf in order to:

  • receive feedback through QR codes at the Customer’s places,
  • sort, route and alert on cases to the right users,
  • send confirmation and replies to the person who gave feedback,
  • produce statistics, reports and, if the Customer chose the Insight plan, AI summaries and suggested replies.

3. Data subjects and personal data

Data subjects: people giving feedback, the Customer’s users with accounts, and the Customer’s employees if the Customer has enabled shift linking.

  • Feedback: email address, optional name, message, optional photo, rating, topic, place code and time.
  • Technical data for spam and abuse protection: truncated IP address and browser type, kept for at most 30 days.
  • Users: name, email, role and login log.
  • Shifts (only if enabled): vehicle, place and time linked to an internal staff ID.

The service is not intended for special categories of data under Article 9 GDPR, criminal offence data or national ID numbers. The form asks people not to share such data and not to photograph other people. The Customer must not design topics or questions that ask for such data.

4. Duration and retention

Processing lasts as long as the main agreement. Feedback is deleted automatically after the retention period the Customer chooses (6–24 months, default 12). After that only anonymous statistics remain.

5. The Customer’s instructions

ScanSay processes personal data only on the Customer’s documented instructions, meaning this agreement and the settings the Customer makes in the service. ScanSay informs the Customer immediately if it believes an instruction breaks data protection law.

ScanSay does not process the data for its own purposes, never sells it and does not use it to train AI models.

6. Confidentiality

Everyone working for ScanSay with access to the personal data is bound by written confidentiality and may only process it to deliver the service.

7. Security

ScanSay takes technical and organisational measures under Article 32 GDPR. They are described in Annex 2 and reviewed at least once a year.

8. Sub-processors

The Customer gives general prior authorisation for the sub-processors in Annex 3. ScanSay notifies the Customer at least 30 days before adding or replacing one. The Customer may object on reasonable grounds and, if no solution is found, terminate the service at no cost.

ScanSay signs written agreements with sub-processors containing the same obligations as this agreement and remains fully liable to the Customer for their processing.

9. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

All personal data is stored and processed in the EU/EEA, including by sub-processors and in AI features. Transfer to a third country may only happen with the Customer’s written approval and with safeguards under Chapter V GDPR.

10. Data subject rights

ScanSay helps the Customer answer requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, including with export and deletion tools in the service. A request received directly by ScanSay is forwarded to the Customer without undue delay.

11. Personal data breaches

ScanSay notifies the Customer without undue delay, and no later than 24 hours after discovering a breach. The notice contains the information needed under Article 33(3) GDPR, so the Customer can report to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) within 72 hours and inform data subjects where required.

12. Impact assessment

ScanSay assists the Customer with information for a data protection impact assessment and any prior consultation with IMY under Articles 35–36 GDPR.

13. Staff and employment law

Named staff statistics are off by default. The Customer is responsible for union consultation under MBL, informing employees and having a legal basis before enabling the feature. The service makes no automated decisions about employees under Article 22 GDPR, and the AI features never assess individual employees.

14. Public sector

If the Customer is a public authority, municipality or region, feedback may become official documents. The Customer is responsible for the Public Access to Information and Secrecy Act (OSL) and archiving rules. ScanSay helps with export in a readable format.

15. When the agreement ends

The Customer can export its data for 30 days after the agreement ends. ScanSay then deletes all personal data within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days. ScanSay confirms deletion in writing on request.

16. Audits

ScanSay gives the Customer the information needed to show compliance with this agreement. The Customer, or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality, may audit once a year with 30 days’ notice, and additionally after a breach or at IMY’s request.

17. Liability, changes and governing law

Liability follows Article 82 GDPR and the main agreement. Changes must be in writing. Swedish law applies and disputes are settled by Swedish courts, with Gothenburg District Court as first instance.

Reviews in 2026: the numbers every hotel, restaurant and taxi firm should know

Guests read more reviews than ever, and they want them to be recent. Here are the key numbers from this year’s major consumer survey and from research, with sources.

More people read reviews, and expectations are rising

In BrightLocal’s Local Consumer Review Survey 2026, covering 1,002 US adults, 41% say they always read reviews when looking for a business, up from 29% the year before. 85% say positive reviews make them more likely to choose a business, and 77% are put off by negative ones.

41%always read reviews (29% the year before)
31%only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or higher (17% the year before)
74%look for reviews from the last three months

The bar has risen too. 31% only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or more, compared with 17% the year before. The gap between 4.2 and 4.6 isn’t cosmetic. It decides whether you make the shortlist at all.

Recency beats volume

74% look for reviews from the last three months, and 32% want reviews from the last two weeks. A great rating from three years ago doesn’t help much today. What counts is how you’re experienced right now.

Your replies are read too

80% prefer a business that responds to every review, but 50% are put off by templated replies. For hotels, a PhoCusWright survey for Tripadvisor (12,000 respondents, 2013) found that 80% read 6–12 reviews before choosing where to stay, and 62% would rather book a hotel whose management responds to reviews.

More reviews sell, but perfect looks suspicious

Northwestern University’s Spiegel Research Center found that purchase likelihood is 270% higher for a product with five reviews than for one with none. Reviews from verified buyers increased purchase likelihood by 15%.

  • Problems should be fixed while the guest is still there, before they become a public review.

What is a star worth? How ratings affect price, revenue and profit

A rating isn’t just a number on a map. Research from Harvard and Cornell shows it can be measured in money. Here are the studies and three worked examples.

Restaurants: 5–9% per star

Michael Luca at Harvard Business School compared restaurants’ Yelp ratings with tax data from Washington State. A one-star increase was associated with 5–9% higher revenue for independent restaurants. Chains showed no such effect, probably because guests already know what they’ll get.

Hotels: 11.2% higher price per point

Chris Anderson at Cornell University (2012) found that a hotel raising its review score by one point on a five-point scale can raise its price by 11.2% and keep the same occupancy. When ReviewPro’s Global Review Index rose by 1%, average daily rate rose by up to 0.89%, occupancy by up to 0.54% and revenue per available room (RevPAR) by up to 1.42%.

5–9%higher revenue per star, independent restaurants (Harvard 2011)
11.2%higher price per point, hotels (Cornell 2012)
1.42%higher RevPAR per 1% better review index (Cornell 2012)

Loyalty is cheaper than new customers

Fred Reichheld of Bain & Company, who created the Net Promoter Score, showed that small improvements in how many customers stay have large effects. In financial services, a 5% increase in customer retention produced more than a 25% increase in profit. The figure comes from another industry, but the mechanism is the same: a returning guest costs less to keep than a new one costs to win.

Three worked examples

These examples are simplified and based on the studies above. They aren’t promises, but they show the order of magnitude.

A 60-room hotel

An average rate of SEK 1,200 and 70% occupancy gives room revenue of about SEK 18.4 million a year (60 × 365 × 0.70 × 1,200). If the rating rises by 0.3 points, Cornell’s finding corresponds to about 3.4% higher prices (0.3 × 11.2%). That’s roughly SEK 620,000 more a year at the same occupancy.

An independent restaurant

Turnover of SEK 8 million a year. Half a star more corresponds, per the Harvard study, to 2.5–4.5% higher revenue: SEK 200,000–360,000 more a year.

A taxi firm with 20 cars

There’s no equivalent study for taxis. If each car turns over SEK 60,000 a month, that’s SEK 14.4 million a year. If fewer unhappy customers means just 1% more choose you again, that’s SEK 144,000 more a year.

The cost of not listening

The most expensive feedback is the feedback you never get. Every problem not reported on site risks becoming a review that stays up for years, and a guest who never returns. That’s why ScanSay is built to catch problems while they can still be fixed.

Fake reviews are banned. That’s why we verify every message

A feedback system is only worth something if you can trust what comes in. That’s why every ScanSay message must be confirmed with a one-time code by SMS or email before it’s sent.

EU rules on reviews

Since 28 May 2022 the EU Omnibus Directive (2019/2161) has applied in full. It bans businesses from writing or commissioning fake reviews and from manipulating reviews, for example by only showing positive ones. Anyone displaying reviews must also say whether and how they check that reviews come from real customers. Claiming reviews are verified without taking reasonable steps to check them is an unfair commercial practice.

Verification builds trust

The Spiegel Research Center found that a verified buyer badge increased purchase likelihood by 15%. People trust feedback more when they know the sender has been checked. The same applies to internal feedback: a manager makes better decisions when the inbox isn’t full of junk.

How verification works in ScanSay

  1. The guest scans the QR code and writes a message.
  2. The guest chooses SMS or email and receives a six-digit code.
  3. The code is valid for 10 minutes and can only be used once. After five wrong attempts the guest must request a new code.
  4. Only when the code matches is the message sent, marked Verified.

More protection against spam

  • One message per person, place and day.
  • At most three codes per phone number or email address per hour.
  • An invisible bot trap, a timing check and an in-browser calculation that makes mass sending expensive.
  • Throwaway addresses and email domains that can’t receive mail are blocked.
  • All checks are repeated on the server, and every QR code is signed so a fake sticker won’t work.

Privacy first

The phone number or email address is only used for the code and to reply to the guest. Staff never see it. Data is stored in the EU. Guests can still stay anonymous to the business by leaving the name blank, but not to the spam protection.

ScanSay doesn’t publish reviews. Verification is about letting the business trust its own feedback, and making sure certificates and statistics are based on real guests.
