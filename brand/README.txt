ScanSay brand (November 2026)

SLOGAN
  Swedish:  Skanna. Säg det.
  English:  Scan it. Say it.

LOGO
A speech bubble made of a QR code: the bubble is the "say", the QR modules are the "scan".
The wordmark is one connected word, "ScanSay", with "Say" in the sunset gradient.
All text is converted to outlines, so no fonts are needed.
